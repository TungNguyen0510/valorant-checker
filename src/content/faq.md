## How it works?

The Valorant Skin Checker works by securely connecting to the Riot Games API. Here is the step-by-step process:

1. **Authentication**: You log in using your Riot Games account credentials. We use a secure authentication flow that never stores your password.
2. **Data Retrieval**: Once authenticated, we fetch your account's skin inventory, including weapon skins, buddies, and player cards.
3. **Display**: The application parses this data and displays it in a clean, organized dashboard, allowing you to browse your collection effortlessly.
4. **Security**: All API requests are encrypted, and we follow Riot Games' security best practices to ensure your account remains safe.

## Is it safe to use?

Yes! We use standard authentication methods and do not store any sensitive account information. We only read the data necessary to display your skins.

## Do you store my Riot password?

No. We never see or store your password. The authentication is handled through a secure token-based system.

## Can I get banned for using this?

No. This tool is a read-only viewer for your inventory. It does not modify game files or provide any competitive advantage.

## How often is the data updated?

The data is updated in real-time whenever you refresh the dashboard.
