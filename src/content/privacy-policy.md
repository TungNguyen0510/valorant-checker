# Privacy Policy

Welcome to Valorant Checker. Your privacy is important to us.

## 1. Information We Collect
We collect information that you provide directly to us when you use our service, including:
- Your Riot ID and Tagline.
- Your Valorant account data (skins, match history, etc.) retrieved via Riot APIs.
- Technical data such as IP address and browser information.

## 2. How We Use Your Information
We use the collected information to:
- Provide and maintain our service.
- Display your Valorant account information.
- Improve our application and user experience.

## 3. Data Storage
Your account information and session data may be stored using secure cloud services and databases.

## 4. Third-Party Services
We use third-party services like Clerk for authentication. Please refer to their respective privacy policies for more information.

## 5. Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support.valorantchecker@gmail.com.
